# 🧠 MentalWatch

MentalWatch is an AI-powered Mental Health Monitoring System designed to analyze user-generated text and identify potential signs of mental distress, depression, anxiety, and suicidal ideation. The system leverages Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning techniques to provide early risk assessment and support mental health awareness.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Mental health issues often go unnoticed until they become severe. MentalWatch aims to assist in the early detection of mental health deterioration by analyzing textual data and identifying patterns associated with emotional distress.

The system classifies users into different mental health risk categories based on the content of their journal entries, social posts, or textual inputs.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional mental health assessments require professional intervention and may not always be accessible.

MentalWatch aims to:

* Detect early signs of mental health deterioration.
* Analyze textual expressions using NLP.
* Classify mental health risk levels.
* Support data-driven mental health monitoring.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* Mental health risk prediction using Machine Learning.
* NLP-based text preprocessing and analysis.
* MentalBERT-based semantic feature extraction.
* PCA-based dimensionality reduction.
* XGBoost classification model.

* Multi-class risk categorization.

## 📊 Dataset Information

The model was trained using a large-scale mental health dataset collected from multiple Reddit communities related to:

* Anxiety
* Depression
* Mental Health
* Loneliness
* SuicideWatch

The dataset was processed and categorized into multiple mental health severity levels for supervised learning.
